Terms of use

FieldCall lets signed-in users record outdoor event plans, define their own weather limits, and compare those limits with a source-linked forecast.

You are responsible for checking that the selected event location, coordinates, time zone, and event hours are correct. Google Maps address results can be ambiguous; review the resolved coordinates before saving.

Forecasts can be incomplete, delayed, unavailable, or inaccurate. FieldCall comparisons are informational only. They are not weather alerts, safety advice, or a substitute for official warnings or your own risk assessment.

Use FieldCall lawfully and do not submit information you are not authorized to use. Google Maps Geocoding, MET Norway, Google sign-in, and hosting are provided by third parties and remain subject to their own service terms.

FieldCall is provided as-is for hackathon evaluation. No continuous availability or forecast accuracy is promised.

For project questions, contact the project through the FieldCall GitHub repository.
